Engineering Deep Dive

Under the Hood: Precision Math Solvers

Mathematical formulas engineered for edge-case accuracy and syllabus dynamics.

1. Dynamic Weighted Normalization

When a semester is still ongoing and weights sum to less than 100%, GradeMaster normalizes standing based on completed work:

Score = (∑ (Score_i × Weight_i)) / (∑ Weight_i) × 100

Alerts in real-time if total weight exceeds 100% or is missing allocations.

2. Inverse Linear Final Exam Solver

Solves for the exact score needed on a weighted final exam:

Exam_{req} = [Target - Current × (1 - W_{exam})] / W_{exam}

Categorizes outcomes into: Secured (≤0%), Achievable (≤85%), Challenging (≤100%), or Extra Credit (>100%).
